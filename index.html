The problem

Geothermal is flying blind.

An industry worth trillions operates with spreadsheets, legacy seismic tools built for oil & gas, and geological intuition. That ends now.

No purpose-built software stack
Geothermal developers stitch together tools designed for petroleum, academic GIS platforms, and manual data analysis. Nothing was built for this use case.
Blind site selection costs millions
A dry or underperforming well can cost $10–30M. Current site selection relies on sparse geological surveys and expert guesswork, with no AI synthesis layer.
Seismicity risk is unmodelled in real time
Induced seismicity from injection operations causes project shutdowns and community opposition. Prediction models exist in academia — not in operator workflows.
Reservoir data lives in silos
Well logs, seismic surveys, temperature gradients, and injection data are fragmented across formats and teams. No unified intelligence layer exists to synthesise them.

Quantrel is the first AI-native software platform built exclusively for geothermal and deep earth energy. It connects geological data to decisions — at every stage of a project's life.

Module 01

Site Selection

  • Heat gradient and permeability modelling
  • Integration with public geological databases
Module 02
DrillIQ™
Drilling Optimisation
Real-time AI advisory during drilling operations. Reduce NPT, optimise ROP, predict formation hazards before you hit them.
  • Real-time drilling parameter optimisation
  • Formation hazard prediction from LWD signals

ReservoirOS™
Performance Modelling

  • Production decline analysis and forecasting
  • EGS stimulation design optimization

  • Real-time microseismic event monitoring
  • Traffic light protocol automation

How it works

From raw data to
drill decision
in hours.

Quantrel connects to your existing data sources and delivers structured intelligence — not another dashboard.

01
Ingest your geological dataset
Connect seismic surveys, well log archives, gravity and magnetics data, surface heat flow measurements, and injection records — in any format.
02
AI models synthesise and score
Quantrel's geothermal-trained ML models run multi-physics analysis: subsurface temperature gradients, permeability proxies, structural fault mapping, and play fairway scoring.
